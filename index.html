The science

How does it work?

  1. 1

    Cleanse

    Soap molecules have a water-loving head and an oil-loving tail. They surround dirt and oil so water can rinse them away.

  2. 2

    Exfoliate

    Papain is a protease. It cuts the keratin bonds that hold dead surface cells together, so they loosen and rinse off.

  3. 3

    Brighten

    With the dull layer gone, fresher skin shows through. Vitamin C and antioxidants help even the look of skin tone.

  4. 4

    Moisturize

    Glycerin attracts water and natural oils soften, so skin feels smooth after rinsing.

Cleanse + Exfoliate + Moisturize = Fresher, brighter-looking skin
